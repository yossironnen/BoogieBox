AI-powered mixing

Meet BoogieMix, your experimental AI DJ.

Go beyond shuffle. BoogieMix uses AI-assisted planning and local music analysis to choose track order, plan transitions, and render continuous listening sessions from the playlists you already love.

  • AI-assisted mix and transition planning
  • Stem-aware crossfades shaped by real drum, bass, and vocal activity
  • Harmonic key compatibility via Camelot detection
  • Precise vocal cue points so transitions never cut mid-lyric
  • Beat-phase alignment using neural beat-grid analysis
  • Spectral energy profiling for smoother playlist flow
  • Downloadable rendered mixes

New — playback bar

Waveform visualizer.

The playback bar now shows a live waveform visualizer, giving you an at-a-glance view of the track's energy as it plays.

New — stem analysis

Sonic Fingerprint.

Every deeply analyzed track gets a Sonic Fingerprint — a live stem breakdown visible right in the player. See what's happening at the instrument level and understand exactly where BoogieMix will place its transitions.

  • Per-stem heatmap rows for vocals, drums, bass, and other
  • Section markers and transition windows overlaid on the waveform bar
  • BPM, harmonic key, section type, and confidence badge at a glance
  • Filter Browse and Search to tracks with real stem data
  • Live ANALYZING badge while a track is being processed

The useful details

Serious library tools. A little personality.

Playback

MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, OPUS, WAV, AAC, WMA, AIFF, and APE.

Analysis

Waveforms, BPM detection, Sonic Fingerprint stem analysis, listening history, and optional BoogieMix deep analysis with Demucs.

Connected

Last.fm, lyrics, metadata enrichment, mobile access, and optional DLNA/UPnP.

Built with

Rust, React, TypeScript, Tauri 2, SQLite, and FFmpeg.
